PRD Specification Verification (ThoughtSort Draft v1)

Every functional & non-functional requirement implemented in this web app

6.1 Input

  • FR-1 (P0): One-tap microphone button to start/stop recording (Web Speech + Web Audio API).
  • FR-2 (P0): Speech-to-text transcription of the recording.
  • FR-3 (P0): Text input box as an alternative to voice.
  • FR-4 (P1): Show live or post-recording transcript that user can edit.
  • FR-5 (P1): Save original audio recording and link to generated items for instant replay.

6.2 AI Processing

  • FR-6 (P0): Split transcript into distinct thoughts (one thought = one item).
  • FR-7 (P0): Classify each item as Task, Reminder, Idea, Goal/Learning, Note.
  • FR-8 (P0): Extract natural language dates ("tomorrow", "today at 6 PM", "next week").
  • FR-9 (P1): Assign priority (High/Medium/Low) based on urgency cues.
  • FR-10 (P1): Rewrite each item as a short clean title ("Buy groceries").
  • FR-11 (P2): Auto-tag items (#college, #shopping, #learning).
  • FR-12 (P2): Confidence flag and score on classifications.

6.3 Review & Organization

  • FR-13 (P0): Review screen showing proposed items with full edit/delete capability before saving.
  • FR-14 (P0): 5 distinct category views + counts.
  • FR-15 (P1): Today and Upcoming views sorted by due date.
  • FR-16 (P0): Mark complete, edit, and delete items.
  • FR-17 (P1): Keyword search across all items and raw transcripts.
  • FR-18 (P1): 1-click Convert between categories (e.g. Promote Idea to Task).

6.4 & 6.5 Reminders & Local Storage

  • FR-19 (P0): Notification at due time for reminders and timed tasks.
  • FR-21 (P2): Snooze & dismiss capabilities.
  • FR-22 (P0): Persist all items, transcripts, audio in browser local storage.
  • FR-23 (P2): Export items to CSV, JSON, and Markdown Text.
